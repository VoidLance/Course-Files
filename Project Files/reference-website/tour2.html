Sunset Cruise

Experience the magic of the ocean at dusk with our Sunset Cruise. Glide across calm waters as the sky transforms into a canvas of vibrant colors, enjoying the tranquil atmosphere and breathtaking views.

  • Duration: 2.5 hours
  • Departure: Daily at 5:30 PM
  • Highlights: Panoramic sunset views, coastal sightseeing, relaxing music, complimentary drinks
  • Includes: Yacht cruise, welcome drink, light snacks, music, expert crew
  • Price: $65 per person

Perfect for couples, families, and friends, this cruise offers a serene escape and a memorable way to end your day. Reserve your spot for an evening to remember!
